BATTLE ANIMATION FAKE HEALTH
=============================
Status: PLANNING ONLY — not being implemented yet.

THE IDEA
--------
Make the health bar drop in a dramatic, satisfying way instead of just
snapping into a fixed 500ms tween the instant the game state updates.

CURRENT STATE
-------------
EnemyZone.jsx renders the health bar's width bound directly to the real
`enemy.health` value, eased via a plain CSS `transition-all duration-500`
(EnemyZone.jsx:160/169). The REAL health number updates the instant the
battle step resolves the action (game logic needs the true value right
away — death checks, next-action calculations, etc. all depend on it).
The visual bar just eases toward whatever the new number is over a fixed
500ms, starting the moment state changes — completely independent of
when the attack's impact frame / PIXI actually visually lands on screen.

THE IDEA — A SEPARATE "FAKE" HEALTH BAR
-------------------------------------------
Real health stays exactly as-is — updates instantly, drives all game
logic, never touches this. What changes is what gets RENDERED:

Introduce a second, purely-visual "fake" health value that's what the bar
actually displays. Instead of tweening the instant the real number
changes, the fake bar's drop gets its own timing — synced to the
timeline (start), so it visually drops when the hit actually connects
on screen, not the instant the reducer does the math. Purely about drama/
feel, not a gameplay mechanic.

Same pattern floatingNumber already uses (config.floatingNumber can be an
array, each entry with its own `delay`/`split`, staggering when each
piece of damage gets SHOWN) — worth reusing that shape for the fake
health bar instead of inventing a new one.

OPEN QUESTIONS / NOT YET DECIDED
---------------------------------
  - Where does the fake value live — local state in EnemyZone/
    PlayerPortrait, or something threaded through the animation timeline
    like floatingNumber is?
  - Multi-hit attacks (dual_heavy_slice, kuzu_ryusen, etc.) — does the
    fake bar step down once per hit in sync with each impact, or just
    once at the end?
  - Any easing/motion beyond timing sync worth exploring for "dramatic"
    (e.g. overshoot then settle, a brief flash on the bar itself)?
